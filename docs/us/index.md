Governments in the United States are organized in layers: the national government, 50 state governments, county governments (called parishes in Louisiana and boroughs in Alaska), municipalities (cities, towns, villages, townships) and thousands of special-purpose districts (school, hospital, flood-control, port, utility and transit districts among them). Each layer sets its own budget and, for most of them, its own property or sales tax rate. govspends files each government under its state and level, so the same questions can be asked of any of them.
