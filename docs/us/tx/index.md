Texas local governments budget on their own fiscal years and set property-tax rates under the state's truth-in-taxation rules (Tax Code chapter 26), which cap the growth of maintenance-and-operations revenue from existing property at 3.5% a year without a voter-approval election for most cities and counties. County budgets are adopted by the Commissioners Court; the County Auditor, appointed by the district judges, certifies the resources available.
